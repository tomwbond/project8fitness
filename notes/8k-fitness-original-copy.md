## About 8K Fitness

At 8K Fitness, we believe everyone deserves to feel stronger, move better, and enjoy being active - regardless of their age, ability, or experience. We're more than just a fitness class. We're a welcoming community where people come together to build confidence, improve performance, and support one another through every stage of their fitness journey.

Our sessions combine expert Strength & Conditioning coaching with functional movement, mobility, and fitness to help you become stronger, more resilient, and better equipped for everyday life. Whether your goal is to run your first 5K, improve your sporting performance, reduce your risk of injury, or simply feel healthier, we're here to help.

Founded by Stephen, a Strength & Conditioning Coach with over 10 years of experience across specialist healthcare and the fitness industry, 8K Fitness is built on the belief that movement should be accessible, inclusive, and enjoyable. Our coaching is designed to help every individual understand their body, build confidence, and reach their full potential in a supportive environment.

With small group training, personalised coaching, educational workshops, and a genuine sense of community, 8K Fitness isn't just about training harder—it's about moving better, living stronger, and enjoying the journey together.

**Train Hard. Live Easy.**
