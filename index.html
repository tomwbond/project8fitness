What our classes are like

Everyone deserves to feel stronger, move better, and enjoy being active, whatever their age, ability, or experience.

We’re more than a fitness class: we’re a welcoming community where people build confidence and support each other.

Each session is different. We build useful strength, cultivate mobility, and improve fitness through fun, challenging circuits. We work hard, rest, then go again.

We draw on a range of disciplines, and every exercise scales up or down, so you work at your own level. Every session is coached, not just led, so you’ll get pointers on your form and plenty of encouragement.

Outdoors

Bootcamp-style classes with bodyweight work, running, and partner drills in every season.

Indoors

The same hard work with less running and more shelter, plus the occasional slower, mobility-focused session.

Your first session

  • Arrive 10 minutes early so we can say hello.
  • Tell us about any injuries or health conditions before we start.
  • Bring water (and a yoga or camping mat for indoor classes).
  • Wear layers you can move in, and gloves in the winter.
  • Trainers with grip if it’s wet out.
