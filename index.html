What our classes are like

We believe everyone deserves to feel stronger, move better, and enjoy being active, whatever their age, ability, or experience.

Each session is different. We build useful strength, cultivate mobility, and improve fitness through fun and challenging circuits. We work hard, rest, then go again.

Our classes use ideas from a range of disciplines and include options to scale each exercise up or down, so you’ll be exercising at your own level.

Outdoors

Bootcamp-style classes on Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday. Bodyweight work, running, and partner drills in every season.

Indoors

Wednesday evening at Ashenground Community Centre. The same hard work, but with less running and more shelter. From time to time, indoor sessions become slower, more targeted, and mobility-focused.

Your first session

  • Arrive 10 minutes early so we can say hello.
  • Tell us about any injuries or health conditions before we start.
  • Bring water (and a yoga or camping mat for indoor classes).
  • Wear layers you can move in, and gloves in the winter.
  • Trainers with grip if it’s wet out.
